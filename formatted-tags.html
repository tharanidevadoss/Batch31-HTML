What's the difference between designing a men's T-shirt and women's T-shirt?
The differences between men's and women's T-shirts lies in the physical facets of the design. T-shirts for women are designed to be shorter than those for men. Men's T-shirts are usually intended to be fitted around the chest and then flow loosely from there. Women's T-shirts are meant to flatter the curves and are more form-fitting.
